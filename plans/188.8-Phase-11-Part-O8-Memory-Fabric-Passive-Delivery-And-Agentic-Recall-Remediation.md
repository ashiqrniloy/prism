# Phase 11 Part O8 — Memory fabric passive delivery and agentic recall remediation

## Objectives

- Fix the causes behind 188.7 finding F13 (fabric is −0.2714 against full context on the confirmatory LongMemEval-S pair) that the post-acceptance investigation localized. Most failures happen where the right round is ranked but not delivered, not where nothing relevant is found.
- Make passive injection deliver every top-ranked note inside its snippet budget, rather than one oversized raw round.
- Make the benchmark give the question date to every arm as message time, not as lexical query text, so temporal phrases resolve against the question date.
- Make the agentic arm measurable: recall must be able to reach the kinds that hold the history, and a turn-limit hit must be a scored wrong answer, not the end of the arm.
- Measure the result with a paid, held-out before/after comparison and record honestly what remains (multi-session aggregation, preference vocabulary gap, full-context substitution).

## Expected Outcome

- An offline delivery-stage diagnostic reproduces the investigation's numbers on a frozen development partition and shows the fix effect before any paid run. Measures: query share from date text, all needed rounds visible under the budget, answer-bearing message visible.
- Passive injection renders bounded, query-centred excerpts. With the default `topK: 3` and `budgetTokens: 600`, up to three hits fit instead of about one. The best hit's answer-bearing span stays visible.
- Protocol v2 delivers the question date through the answer message's metadata and timestamp for all arms. `in March` and `last week` resolve against the question date. No date or timestamp tokens enter the passive query.
- `memory.recall` with default kinds reports where matches exist when the default kinds have none. A `RunLimitError` on one agentic question is scored as `run_limit` and the arm continues.
- A paid held-out comparison of pre-fix fabric, post-fix fabric, post-fix fabric-agentic and a full-context reference. It runs under a fresh owner-approved cap and gives an explicit verdict for F13 in 188.7's finding matrix.

This plan implements roadmap Phase 11 Part O's memory-quality requirement as the next 188.x sub-plan. It follows 188.7's incomplete Task 8 campaign without waiting for it to finish, and feeds evidence back to that task's F13 row. It does not complete 188.7 Task 8 or 188.6 Task 6, and it does not rerun their lifecycle, LoCoMo, MemoryAgentBench-CR, `om` or `om+fabric` arms. **No review task:** the plan changes no kernel contract and creates or moves no package. Changes are internal to `@arnilo/prism-memory-fabric` (`context.ts`, `tools.ts`) and to `scripts/memory-bench`. Task 1 is a measurement freeze, not a contract review. It adds no export, config field, dependency, version bump, publish or `/docs` page. `inject` and promotion stay off by default, and lexical retrieval stays the default. A consolidation-built fabric arm (extracted facts instead of raw rounds) is out of scope: its extractor input over a ~115k-token haystack per question costs about as much as the full arm. It is recorded here so plan end can hand it to Further Actions.
